# KAVANDOR — UI Rules & Interaction Guidelines

## 1. Avoid Design Clichés
- **NO generic purple/blue AI gradients:** The brand palette is Electric Orange (`#FF5A1F`), Premium Charcoal (`#17191D`), and Warm Paper (`#F6F3EC`).
- **NO decorative glassmorphism or floating blobs:** Interfaces must feel grounded, solid, and reliable like high-end industrial logistics software.
- **NO emojis in navigation, headers, or status tags:** Use crisp custom SVG pictograms and sharp typography.
- **NO robotic mascots or generic AI avatars:** AI presence is indicated by precise badges, deterministic traces, and crisp status pips (`AI-Assisted (Gemini Vision)` vs `Deterministic Fixture`).

---

## 2. Role-Specific Interface Paradigms

### A. Farmer Workspace (`/farmer`)
- **Philosophy:** Radically simple, high contrast, legible in direct sunlight, minimal cognitive load.
- **Ergonomics:** Big tap targets (min 48px height), prominent CTA ("Report a Harvest"), instant photo preview.
- **Tone:** Reassuring, clear, free of technical logistics jargon.

### B. Transporter Workspace (`/transporter`)
- **Philosophy:** Mobile-first field execution screen.
- **Ergonomics:** One clear next action per screen (e.g. "Arrived at Farm", "Produce Collected", "Delivered at Packhouse").
- **Tone:** Professional, direct, showing explicit earnings in KES.

### C. Facility Operator Workspace (`/facility`)
- **Philosophy:** High-density, fast-scanning packhouse ledger.
- **Ergonomics:** Visual capacity meters, one-click intake modals, barcode/batch tags, quick filter tables.
- **Tone:** Operational, disciplined, discrepancy-aware.

### D. Buyer & Cooperative Workspace (`/buyer`)
- **Philosophy:** Premium institutional commodity procurement.
- **Ergonomics:** Batch card grids, verified-quality seals, multi-batch order combinator.
- **Tone:** Commercially rigorous, transparent, escrow-conscious.

### E. Operations Command Center (`/ops`)
- **Philosophy:** Live mission control.
- **Ergonomics:** Darker elevated charcoal panels (`#17191D`, `#22252A`), real-time event ticker, inspectable agent workflows, actionable exceptions.
- **Tone:** Vigilant, comprehensive, accountable.
