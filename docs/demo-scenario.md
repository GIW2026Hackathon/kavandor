# Demo scenario

## Tomato harvest in Kisii

- Crop: tomato
- Location: Kisii County, Kenya
- Quantity: 220 kg
- Unit: kg
- Assumption: all pricing is demonstration-only and must be re-validated before any live system use

## Simulated flows

1. Farmer logs harvest.
2. System performs a visible-condition assessment.
3. The app compares immediate sale, cold storage, and aggregation options.
4. Buyer receives a demo offer and may accept or reject it.
5. Facility and transport statuses are tracked as a coordinated workflow.
